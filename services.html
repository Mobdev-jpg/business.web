HOW SOCIAL MANAGEMENT WORKS

We run the content rhythm. You stay in control.

You keep ownership of your Instagram, Facebook Page and ad account. ORVEXA receives only the access needed to create and publish approved content. We never need your personal password.

01
Audit & plan

We review your current pages, goals, audience and offers, then agree the platforms and monthly deliverables.

02
Create & approve

We design the posts, edit supplied clips, write captions and send the content calendar for your approval.

03
Publish & engage

Approved content is scheduled. Common comments can be handled using your approved answers; sales enquiries are forwarded to you.

04
Review & improve

You receive a report on reach, engagement, profile actions and enquiries, plus recommendations for the next month.
