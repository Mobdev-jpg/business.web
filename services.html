HOW WE WORK

Clear scope. You stay in control.

  • You own your Instagram, Facebook Page, ad account and audience. ORVEXA requests role-based access; we never need your password or OTP.
  • All work is remote and digital. You upload your photos, video clips, text and brand materials; we return finished designs, edits and website files online. We do not provide on-site recording, photography or in-person visits.
  • Posts and captions are sent for approval, with one consolidated revision round per monthly content batch.
  • Community monitoring is limited to agreed working hours. Sales, refunds, bookings and sensitive queries are passed to the business owner.
  • Advertising budgets, paid third-party tools and external platform charges are separate unless listed in a written quote.
  • No guaranteed follower counts, virality, leads or sales. We report on activity and improve the plan using results.
